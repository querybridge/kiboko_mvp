# Product Update — June 25, 2026

**Prepared for:** Doug
**Summary:** Today we sharpened the language and structure of the planning tool so it's clearer how strategy connects to execution, and made the dashboard easier to read at a glance.

---

## 1. Renamed "Increase Purchase Frequency" → "Increase Close Rate"

The third core objective now reads **Increase Close Rate** everywhere — the dashboard tiles, intake forms, and supporting docs. Existing records were updated automatically, so nothing looks half-renamed.

**Why it matters:** "Close Rate" is the metric the team actually manages to, so the language now matches how we talk about the business.

---

## 2. Simplified and clarified the planning hierarchy

We replaced the old "Annual Rock / Quarterly Rock / Initiative / Project" vocabulary (which overlapped and caused confusion) with a clean three-level structure:

| Level | Time horizon | Measured by | Replaces |
|-------|--------------|-------------|----------|
| **Objective** | Annual | a **KPI** | Annual Rock |
| **Project** | Quarterly | a **Metric** | Quarterly Rock |
| **Action** | Execution | a **Measure** | the old "Project"/work item |

So the chain is now: **Objective → Project → Action.** Each level has its own kind of measurement, which makes it obvious how a day-to-day Action ladders up to an annual Objective.

**Why it matters:** Everyone now has one shared, unambiguous vocabulary. It's clear what "owns" what, and reporting rolls up cleanly from execution to strategy.

---

## 3. Clearer ownership vs. resourcing

Every **Action** now has a single accountable **Owner** plus a **Team** that provides the resources. The dashboard's timeline (Gantt) groups work by Team, so you can see at a glance where the workload is concentrated and who is accountable for each item.

**Why it matters:** Removes the "who actually owns this?" ambiguity and surfaces team capacity/overload.

---

## 4. Color-coded the timeline by Objective

The Gantt timeline bars are now color-coded by the Objective each Action supports:

- 🟢 **Green** — Increase Average Order Value
- 🟡 **Yellow** — Increase Close Rate
- 🔵 **Blue** — Increase Shopper Volume (Visits)

**Why it matters:** You can instantly see how execution effort is distributed across our three core objectives — and spot if one objective is starved for attention.

---

## 5. Reliability fix

Tightened up a few internal pages so they no longer error out in edge cases (e.g., when opened directly rather than navigated to). No visible change — just fewer surprises.

---

## Status

All changes are **live in the working app and verified** — the dashboard, planning pages, and timeline were tested end-to-end after the updates. Demo data was refreshed to reflect the new structure.
