Premium Connection PREMIUM the most accurate, complete data

The gold standard. Reads the customer's raw GA4 → BigQuery export via a service account, so every dashboard is:

  • Unsampled & exact — event-level, no estimation on any date range.
  • No reporting delay — data as soon as it lands in BigQuery.
  • Item / order-level metrics — unlocks Unique SKUs per Order, Cherry Pick Rate and product-category economics that Standard can't compute.
  • Roll-ups across sites — true company-wide totals.

For speed, Kiboko stores a daily summary table (one row of totals per day, no visitor- or order-level records) on its US servers. It is deleted 90 days after the account is cancelled or disabled. See the Privacy Policy.

Standard Connection Google Analytics 4

Data source: the Google Analytics 4 reporting API (aggregated), read live with your Google sign-in. Fast to set up and free — with a few GA4 limitations:

  • Reporting delay — GA4 data can lag roughly 24–48 hours.
  • Estimation / sampling — long date ranges and high-cardinality breakdowns may be sampled.
  • No item/order-level detail — a few metrics show as Premium Tier Required.

Want exact, unsampled, real-time data? See Premium above.

Disconnect BigQuery for ?

This permanently deletes from Kiboko:

  • the daily summary table holding this company's history in Kiboko, and
  • the service-account key Kiboko uses to read your BigQuery data.

It is gone forever and can't be recovered. Your data in BigQuery itself is not affected.

If you connect again, or start a new Kiboko account, Kiboko rebuilds the history from your BigQuery export, up to about 400 days back, as far as your export still holds. Anything your export no longer keeps can't be recovered.
