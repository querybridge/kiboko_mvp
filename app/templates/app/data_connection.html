Standard Connection Google Analytics 4

Data source: the Google Analytics 4 reporting API (aggregated), read live with your Google sign-in. Fast to set up and free — with a few GA4 limitations:

  • Reporting delay — GA4 data can lag roughly 24–48 hours.
  • Estimation / sampling — long date ranges and high-cardinality breakdowns may be sampled.
  • No item/order-level detail — a few metrics show as Premium Tier Required.

Want exact, unsampled, real-time data? See Premium above.
